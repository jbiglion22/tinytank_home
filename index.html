How to play

Easy to start, fun to master

Steer with one thumb. Your cannon does the aiming for you, so you can focus on dodging, collecting and racing the clock.

1

Move with the joystick

Drag the circle at the bottom right. Your tank turns and rolls like a real tank on tracks.

2

Your cannon fires on its own

The barrel turns to the nearest enemy and shoots. Level up mid-battle and pick a power-up.

3

Reach the finish in time

The timer bar on the right keeps shrinking. Grab hourglasses on the road for more time.
