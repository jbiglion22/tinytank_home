Privacy Policy

Tiny Tank (Android app) · Developer: J.Biglion · Effective date: November 15, 2026

In short
  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your phone. We don't receive them.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure ads.

1. Who we are

Tiny Tank ("the app") is a game made by the individual developer J.Biglion ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how. If you have questions, contact us at jbiglion22@gmail.com.

2. Information the developer collects

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information. The app has no sign-up, login, chat or contact form, and it does not send your game data to our servers (we don't run any).

3. Information stored only on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • Game progress: cleared stages, stars, coins, parts you bought, items and treasures found
  • Settings: language, sound effects, music, vibration, joystick position, whether you've seen the opening and the how-to-play guide

This data never leaves your device through us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings. Android's own backup may copy app data to your Google account if you have turned backup on; that is handled by Google under its own policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: rewarded ads you choose to watch (to revive or to double your coins), an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and a native ad on the exit screen. To show, limit and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

InformationPurpose
Advertising ID (Android)Ad delivery, frequency limits, measurement, and personalized ads where allowed
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, fraud prevention, regional rules
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device
Ad interactions (views, taps, rewards earned) and diagnostic dataMeasurement, reporting, fixing ad problems

This information is collected by Google directly, not by us. Data is encrypted in transit. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You can play the whole game without choosing to watch them.

6. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to load ads. The game itself does not need a connection to play.
  • Vibration: for feedback when your tank is hit (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share your personal information. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google, for advertising, as described in section 4.

8. Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data saved on your device stays until you clear app data or uninstall the app. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google's policy above; you can also reset your advertising ID at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your information, we will update this page and change the effective date above.
